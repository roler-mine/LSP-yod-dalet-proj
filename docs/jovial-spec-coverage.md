# JOVIAL Spec Coverage

This tracker is a conservative snapshot of current lexer, parser, AST, semantic,
navigation, diagnostic, and test coverage. It is intended for planning and should
be updated whenever a feature moves from generic parsing or editor-only support
to spec-aware behavior.

Status values used in the table: `not started`, `partial`, `mostly complete`,
`complete`.

| Feature | Lexer | Parser | AST | Semantics | Navigation | Diagnostics | Tests | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ITEM declarations | complete | mostly complete | mostly complete | mostly complete | mostly complete | mostly complete | mostly complete | `ITEM` is tokenized and parsed into `DVar` with `DataItem`; common hover, definition, references, completion, inlay, and type mismatch paths exist. Some declaration attributes are still parsed generically or ignored. |
| CONSTANT ITEM declarations | complete | mostly complete | mostly complete | partial | mostly complete | partial | partial | `CONSTANT ITEM` parses into `DConst` and has a storage/allocation warning, but constant-specific semantic rules remain shallow. |
| TABLE declarations | complete | partial | mostly complete | partial | mostly complete | partial | partial | Several table forms, dimensions, presets, and record fields parse into `TArray`/`TRecord`. Field ownership now keeps same-name fields in different table owners separate for owner-qualified navigation/references, and simple dimension/preset diagnostics exist; specified tables, packing, and full layout rules remain future work. |
| CONSTANT TABLE declarations | partial | partial | partial | partial | mostly complete | partial | partial | Common `CONSTANT TABLE NAME(...)`, typed, and record-body forms parse as readonly `DConst` table data with `JovialConstantTable` metadata. Hover, inlay, document/workspace symbols, and plain assignment diagnostics are covered; full preset/layout and element-assignment semantics remain conservative. |
| BLOCK declarations | complete | partial | partial | partial | mostly complete | partial | partial | `BLOCK` parses into a `DVar` with `DataBlock` and record-like fields. Block fields participate in owner-scoped metadata/navigation foundations, but full block layout and representation semantics are not implemented. |
| TYPE declarations | complete | mostly complete | mostly complete | partial | mostly complete | partial | partial | Builtin and user type declarations are parsed and surfaced in navigation; the semantic type system is still coarse for many JOVIAL rules. |
| PROC definitions | complete | mostly complete | mostly complete | mostly complete | mostly complete | mostly complete | mostly complete | `PROC` declarations with bodies, parameters, return types, `REC`, and `RENT` have broad support; advanced procedure attributes are not complete. |
| FUNCTION definitions | partial | partial | partial | partial | mostly complete | partial | partial | Return-typed `PROC` declarations are treated as functions in metadata and navigation; there is no separate `FUNCTION` keyword path. |
| DEF declarations | complete | mostly complete | mostly complete | partial | mostly complete | partial | partial | `DEF` modifiers and `DEF BEGIN ... END` groups are represented, and exported symbols participate in navigation/import logic. Cross-module precision is still evolving. |
| REF declarations | complete | mostly complete | mostly complete | partial | mostly complete | partial | partial | `REF` modifiers and groups are represented, and imported items/procs suppress common unresolved diagnostics. Full external contract checking is not implemented. |
| COMPOOL modules | complete | mostly complete | mostly complete | mostly complete | mostly complete | mostly complete | mostly complete | COMPOOL definitions are scanned, indexed, persisted, and used for imports, diagnostics, navigation, code actions, and CodeLens counts. |
| !COMPOOL directives | complete | mostly complete | mostly complete | mostly complete | mostly complete | mostly complete | mostly complete | `!COMPOOL` and `!ICOMPOOL` imports are parsed/scanned and drive import visibility, hints, diagnostics, quick fixes, and indexing. |
| ICOPY directives | partial | partial | partial | partial | partial | partial | partial | `ICOPY` now has an explicit include-target/source-map model, resolved dependency edges, reverse include users, unresolved/cyclic diagnostics for detectable cases, and debug-report visibility. Full include expansion and directive semantics remain provisional. |
| DEFINE macros | complete | mostly complete | mostly complete | partial | mostly complete | mostly complete | mostly complete | DEFINE lexing, parsing, expansion, source mapping, macro graph, hover, goto definition, and references exist. Rename/safety semantics are future work. |
| DEFINE parameters | complete | mostly complete | mostly complete | partial | mostly complete | mostly complete | mostly complete | Formal parameters, actuals, macro graph actual display, and arity diagnostics exist. Full list option and substitution semantics remain conservative. |
| Generated names | partial | partial | partial | partial | partial | partial | partial | Apostrophe-containing and quoted identifiers are accepted in common paths, but generated-name rules are not modeled as a distinct semantic feature. |
| STATUS values | partial | mostly complete | mostly complete | partial | mostly complete | partial | mostly complete | `STATUS (V(...))` lists are represented in AST/type metadata, status constants are indexed as `JovialStatusConstant`, and clear duplicate/member/ambiguous cases have diagnostics. Explicit representation rules remain future work. |
| Pointer types | partial | mostly complete | mostly complete | mostly complete | mostly complete | partial | mostly complete | `P` and `P TYPE` forms map to pointer types, with hover/inlay/type display and coarse compatibility support. Pointer conversion and target rules need more diagnostics. |
| Pointer-qualified references | complete | mostly complete | mostly complete | partial | partial | partial | partial | `@` dereference and field-at-pointer syntax parse to `EDeref`/`EAt`; semantic checking exists for typed pointer dereference and fields but is conservative. |
| LOC/NEXT/BIT/BYTE builtins | partial | partial | partial | partial | partial | partial | partial | Builtins are completion/import-safe names; `LOC` and `NEXT` have special semantic typing, while `BIT` and `BYTE` are mostly recognized as builtin calls without precise result rules. |
| Conversion operators | complete | mostly complete | mostly complete | partial | partial | partial | partial | `(* type *) expr` lexes as conversion brackets and parses to `EConvert`; conversion legality and narrowing/widening diagnostics are not complete. |
| Integer formulas | complete | mostly complete | mostly complete | partial | partial | partial | partial | Integer literals and arithmetic expressions parse, with coarse integer compatibility. Width, signedness, overflow, and formula-specific rules are not complete. |
| Float formulas | complete | mostly complete | mostly complete | partial | partial | partial | partial | Float literals and arithmetic parse, but precision and conversion rules are coarse. |
| Fixed formulas | partial | partial | partial | partial | partial | partial | partial | `A` fixed types are displayed/modelled in `Jovial_type`, but semantic behavior is currently folded into coarse float-like handling. |
| Bit formulas | complete | mostly complete | mostly complete | partial | partial | partial | partial | Based bit literals and boolean/bit operators are tokenized/parsed; bit length and operator result rules are only coarse. |
| Character formulas | partial | partial | partial | partial | partial | partial | partial | Quoted text is tokenized as strings and typed by length in semantic checks; full character/string formula rules are not modeled. |
| Status formulas | partial | partial | partial | partial | mostly complete | partial | partial | `V(...)` remains a compatible constructor expression, with membership diagnostics when an expected STATUS context is known and ambiguity warnings when no context disambiguates duplicate value names. Full representation/conversion semantics remain conservative. |
| Table formulas | partial | partial | partial | partial | partial | partial | partial | Indexing, fields, and presets parse and are walked by navigation/semantics, but table formula typing is intentionally shallow. |
| CASE statements | complete | partial | partial | partial | partial | not started | partial | `CASE` parses enough to preserve/check option bodies, but the scrutinee and case indexes are not represented as a dedicated AST statement. |
| FOR loops | complete | mostly complete | mostly complete | partial | partial | partial | partial | `FOR` parses to `SFor` with loop-control typing and loop-depth checks, but complete loop semantics are not implemented. |
| WHILE loops | complete | mostly complete | mostly complete | partial | partial | partial | partial | `WHILE` parses to `SWhile` and participates in loop-depth diagnostics; condition typing remains coarse. |
| EXIT/GOTO/RETURN/ABORT/STOP | complete | mostly complete | partial | mostly complete | partial | mostly complete | partial | Control statements parse and have diagnostics for loop/procedure/label misuse. `EXIT`, `ABORT`, and `STOP` currently reuse call-statement representation. |
| Table presets | complete | partial | partial | partial | partial | partial | partial | Presets parse into `EPreset` for simple forms; detailed preset validation and positioners are not complete. |
| Table dimensions | complete | mostly complete | mostly complete | partial | partial | partial | mostly complete | Dimensions, ranges, and entry-count hints exist for common cases. Bounds, status dimensions, and full validation are incomplete. |
| Specified tables | partial | partial | partial | partial | partial | partial | partial | Common `TABLE ... W entry-size`/`V` record forms and field `POS(startbit,startword)` clauses now have AST representation, hover facts, and shallow diagnostics for missing/invalid/duplicate/out-of-entry positions. Full packing/layout, overlays, and target-specific representation rules remain future work. |
| Table packing | not started | not started | not started | not started | not started | not started | not started | Packing/layout rules are not modeled. |
| OVERLAY declarations | not started | not started | not started | not started | partial | not started | not started | `OVERLAY` is completion/reserved-word-only today; no parser or semantic representation exists. |
| Implementation parameters | partial | partial | partial | partial | partial | partial | partial | Procedure formals and colon-separated out parameters parse, with limited type inference from local declarations. Full implementation-parameter semantics are not modeled. |
| Inline declarations | not started | partial | not started | not started | partial | not started | not started | `INLINE` appears in completion/reserved-word handling and may be swallowed as a generic attribute, but it is not preserved in AST or checked. |
| Readonly declarations | not started | not started | not started | not started | not started | not started | not started | No lexer/parser/semantic support found. |
| Machine-specific subroutines | not started | not started | not started | not started | not started | not started | not started | No dedicated model for machine-specific subroutine declarations or diagnostics found. |
